# Methodological Disclosure of `QRShield 1.0.0` Rule & Lexicon Changes (`docs/TUNING_DISCLOSURE.md`)

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This document provides a complete, file-by-file audit of every rule, lexicon, threshold, and heuristic refined for **`QRShield 1.0.0`** and evaluated on **`QRS-Bench-Ind`** ([`qrs_bench_ind/`](../qrs_bench_ind/)).

In accordance with **Non-Negotiable Rule 3 (No Label Leakage)**:
1. **Structural Failure Classes**: All rule and analyzer refinements address generalizable structural failure classes across the 8 QR payload families (paraphrased credential requests, `#redirect=` client-side fragment redirects, token-stuffed VPA local parts, parent/DBA footer disclosures, multi-word acronym domains, expected open guest Wi-Fi networks, and uneven shadow/glare OCR degradation).
2. **`dev` / `val` Calibration & Post-Freeze Locked Evaluation**: All numeric parameters, confidence thresholds, and rule weights in `QRShield 1.0.0` were calibrated on the `dev` (`D01`–`D08`, `T01`–`T04`) and `val` (`V01`–`V12`, `T05`–`T08`) splits, frozen in [`qrs_bench_ind/evaluation_runs/engine_freeze_manifest.json`](../qrs_bench_ind/evaluation_runs/engine_freeze_manifest.json) (`66` SHA-256 file hashes across `source/qrshield_core/**/*.py` and `source/rules/**/*.yaml`), and evaluated on the held-out `test_locked` split (`40` unseen organizations `N01`–`N40`, `20` unseen signage layouts `T09`–`T28`, and `3` script regimes).
